# AI-Powered Dehallucination and Reliability Enhancement System

# 1. Project Overview

The **AI-Powered Dehallucination and Reliability Enhancement System** is a software system designed to improve the reliability of information generated by Artificial Intelligence applications.

Modern AI systems, especially Large Language Models (LLMs), can sometimes produce information that is incorrect, unsupported, misleading, or presented with excessive confidence. These incorrect AI-generated statements are commonly referred to as **AI hallucinations**.

The proposed system acts as a **reliability and verification layer** between an AI application and its users.

The system analyzes AI-generated responses, identifies factual claims, verifies those claims against trusted sources or knowledge bases, evaluates the available evidence, assigns confidence scores, and highlights potentially unreliable information.

The project is designed as a modular and reusable system that can be integrated with AI chatbots, AI assistants, content-generation systems, and other AI-powered applications.

---

# 2. Problem Statement

Artificial Intelligence systems are increasingly being used for education, research, business, customer support, content generation, and decision support.

However, AI-generated responses may contain:

- Incorrect facts
- Fabricated information
- Unsupported statements
- Incorrect dates or numbers
- Misleading explanations
- Incorrect references or citations
- Contradictory information

One of the major challenges is that these responses can appear very convincing even when they are incorrect.

Users may therefore trust and use inaccurate AI-generated information without realizing that it is unreliable.

There is a need for a software system that can automatically analyze AI-generated responses and provide a transparent indication of the reliability of their factual claims.

---

# 3. Proposed Solution

The proposed system provides a **verification layer for AI-generated content**.

Instead of directly displaying an AI-generated response to the user, the response is passed through the proposed reliability system.

The system will:

1. Accept an AI-generated response.
2. Preprocess and analyze the response.
3. Identify factual claims.
4. Retrieve relevant information from trusted sources or configured knowledge bases.
5. Compare the generated claims with the retrieved evidence.
6. Determine whether individual claims are supported, unsupported, contradictory, or require further verification.
7. Calculate a confidence score.
8. Highlight potentially hallucinated information.
9. Display supporting or conflicting evidence where available.
10. Generate a verification summary or report.
11. Provide an API interface so the system can be integrated with other AI applications.
